# User Testing Protocol & Clinical Validation Plan

## 1. Intended User Cohorts

SolluPath is designed for individuals who experience temporary, intermittent, or permanent communication barriers:
- **Aphasia & Stroke Recovery**: Individuals who comprehend language but experience verbal expressive motor difficulties.
- **ICU / Post-Operative Patients**: Patients with tracheostomies, endotracheal intubation, or acute vocal fatigue.
- **Cerebral Palsy & Motor Neuropathies**: Users requiring large touch targets and forgiving selection areas.
- **Autism Spectrum & Non-Speaking Communicators**: Users who benefit from visual semantic cards and predictable communication workflows.
- **Caregivers & Healthcare Staff**: Nurses, transit operators, and family members needing immediate two-way clarity.

## 2. Structured Testing Protocol (Single-Subject & Cohort Sessions)

### Pre-Flight Preparation
1. Ensure the device is configured in the participant's primary language (e.g. Tamil or English).
2. Set appropriate text size and audio feedback in Accessibility Settings.
3. Obtain documented participant/guardian informed consent emphasizing privacy and the voluntary nature of testing.

### Scenario Task Battery

| Task ID | Environment | User Prompt / Scenario | Success Metric | Target Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T-01** | Emergency / Clinical | Express: *"I am in pain and I need my caregiver."* | Both cards selected in Sentence Builder | < 15 seconds |
| **T-02** | Clinic / Hospital | Show message to nurse in Large Presentation Mode. | Presentation Mode opened with high contrast | < 5 seconds |
| **T-03** | Public Transit | Inquire: *"Where is this bus going?"* | Transit situation card found and added | < 15 seconds |
| **T-04** | Pharmacy | Request: *"Please explain these instructions."* | Pharmacy category searched and phrase added | < 12 seconds |
| **T-05** | Multilingual | Switch output communication language from Tamil to Hindi. | Message preserved and translated correctly | < 8 seconds |
| **T-06** | Custom Need | Add a personalized phrase: *"My daughter's number is 9876543210"* | Custom phrase saved and selectable | < 30 seconds |

## 3. Quantitative & Qualitative Evaluation Metrics
- **Task Completion Rate (TCR)**: Percentage of scenarios completed without verbal prodding.
- **Time to First Word (TTFW)**: Latency from opening app to placing first phrase in builder.
- **Touch Target Accuracy**: Frequency of accidental mis-taps.
- **Caregiver Comprehension Score**: 1-5 rating on how immediately the receiving caregiver understood the user's intent.
- **System Usability Scale (SUS)**: Target benchmark of SUS score > 85.
